## Full description (max 4000 chars)

⚡ TROLLEY BUS DRIVER ⚡

A simple, addictive arcade game about driving a trolleybus. Your job: stay
locked onto the overhead wire — the longer you hold it, the faster your
score climbs!

HOW TO PLAY
🚎 One-thumb controls — tap the left or right side of the screen to switch
lanes.
⚡ Stay under the power line to rack up points and light up your bus with
a neon glow.
🔌 Lost contact? Tap fast so the driver can reattach the harness before
you lose a life.

RISING DIFFICULTY, ENDLESS STAGES
The game never ends — every stage gets longer, faster, and harder:
• At first — simple lane changes under the wire.
• Later, switches controlled by a lever come into play.
• On higher stages, obstacles block switch branches — you'll really have
  to think about which way to flip the lever!

EXTRA FEATURES
🏆 Local leaderboard of the best drivers
⏸️ Pause anytime — your progress is saved, pick up right where you left
   off
🎵 Looping retro/chiptune soundtrack
🔇 Full sound control

PRIVACY
The game works fully offline — no ads, no sign-up, no data collection.
Everything (score, leaderboard, saved stage) stays on your device only.

Hop in the driver's seat and catch the current! 🚎⚡
